# FREEDAM DAO Code of Conduct

> *"Dissent Stay Decent & Decentralize"*

FREEDAM is a non-partisan, open-source DAO building permissionless infrastructure for civic governance. This Code of Conduct governs all spaces — GitHub, X, Discord, working groups, IRL events, and any forum where FREEDAM members gather.

This document is derived from the [FREEDAM Constitution](CONSTITUTION.md). Where the Code of Conduct and Constitution conflict, the Constitution prevails.

---

## Preamble

FREEDAM exists to restore democratic participation through blockchain-native governance. Our mission is civic, not commercial. Our community is built on the principle that **the best ideas win when everyone has a voice**.

This Code of Conduct is not a restriction. It is a **commitment** — to each other and to the values that make decentralized governance possible.

---

## Core Principles

Derived from the Constitution (Article II):

1. **Permissionless participation.** Any individual may join. No gatekeeper — membership is minted with a small donation to the DAO treasury, and the allowlist phase (first 20 pilot members) is temporary by design.
2. **One person, one voice.** Governance rights are tied to identity, not capital.
3. **Anti-plutocratic.** Influence must reflect conviction, not wealth.
4. **Transparent execution.** Every decision and treasury movement is publicly verifiable.
5. **Non-partisan at the protocol level.** The DAO endorses no candidate, party, or campaign.
6. **Open source, forkable.** All code and documents are published under MIT. The DAO is designed to be copied.
7. **Fair launch.** No presale, no VC allocation, no founder reserve.

## Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Debate ideas vigorously, treat people with dignity.** Critique the argument, not the person.
- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements are misunderstandings, not malice.
- **Respect boundaries.** Not everyone has the same time zone, availability, or comfort level.
- **Credit others.** FREEDAM is built in public — attribute work and give credit where it is due.
- **Protect the community.** If you see a violation, report it. If you made a mistake, own it.

### Unacceptable Behavior

- **Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech** of any kind — including but not limited to race, gender, sexuality, religion, disability, or political affiliation.
- **Threats or intimidation** — whether online or in person.
- **Spam or exploitation** of governance or community spaces.
- **Impersonation** of DAO members, representatives, or working group leads.
- **Coordinated manipulation** of governance processes — including Sybil attacks, vote buying, or bribery.
- **Doxxing** — publishing private information without consent.
- **Behavior that violates applicable law** — including illegal campaign activity or financial fraud.

## Governance Violations

Certain violations are particularly harmful to a DAO's integrity:

| Violation                                                      | Consequence                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Sybil attack (creating multiple identities to influence votes) | FRDM-ID freeze and/or revocation by DAO vote |
| Vote buying or selling                                         | FRDM-ID revocation + public disclosure       |
| Bribery of DAO representatives                                 | FRDM-ID revocation + legal action            |
| Coordinated governance manipulation                            | Permanent ban by DAO vote                    |
| Impersonation of a DAO member on-chain                         | FRDM-ID revocation                           |

These follow the revocation process defined in the Constitution (Article III): revocation requires a passed DAO vote in verified cases of bad faith.

## Agents & Bots

FREEDAM does not ban automation — we require **disclosure**. Wallets are pseudonymous, so the community cannot technically tell a human from a script; honesty is therefore the entry fee.

1. **Disclose before you act.** If you operate a bot, AI agent, or any automated process that interacts with FREEDAM spaces — chat, contributions, proposal drafting, or voting on a member's behalf — state publicly (a) that it is automated and (b) which member operates it. In Discord, a pinned note in #general is sufficient.
2. **One operator stays accountable.** Automated actions are the responsibility of the member who runs them. "The bot did it" is not a defense.
3. **No undisclosed automation.** Operating bots or sock-puppet agents without disclosure is grounds for FRDM-ID revocation under the Governance Violations process above — the contract's revoke function is permanent and public.
4. **Members never lose their vote to automation.** An agent may help you research or draft, but casting your Snapshot vote should be a deliberate human act. Delegate explicitly if you choose to.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a violation:

1. **Direct message** the FREEDAM X account @FREEDAMDAO, or
2. **Email** the Tech Working Group at freedam.dao@zohomail.com

All reports are handled confidentially. The Tech Working Group will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and respond with proposed next steps within 7 days.

**Retaliation against anyone who reports a violation in good faith is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.**

---

## Enforcement

The Tech Working Group, in consultation with the community, is responsible for enforcement. Decisions follow the governance lifecycle defined in the Constitution (Article IV):

1. **Report** received and documented
2. **Review** by the Tech Working Group (72-hour window)
3. **Decision** — warning, temporary suspension, or DAO vote for revocation (Article IX)
4. **Accountability** — record of the decision, with rationale, in the governance log
