How it works

1. Read the plain-language summary and the exact resolution condition.
2. Stake Aura for or against before the stake window closes (staking opens with the first listed market).
3. When the outcome is verified against the resolution source, correct callers get their stake back plus a share of the losing pool. Everyone's calls are on the public record.
