Welcome to FREEDAM DAO

Free Decentralized Autonomous Movement — a non-partisan, open-source civic community. We build tools that make democratic participation accessible, transparent, and verifiable on-chain.

Testnet MVP. Everything below runs on Optimism Sepolia, a test network. The ETH has no real value. The membership roll is real and permanent. This is not a token sale and not an investment.

1 · Mint your FRDM-ID in 3 minutes

A FRDM-ID is your soulbound (non-transferable) membership credential. Each one carries:

  • A sequential member number — Founder = #1, then #2, #3…
  • A membership tier — Member or Leader, set by your donation
  • Voting power — one FRDM-ID, one vote in DAO governance

Step by step

  1. Get a wallet. MetaMask (most popular), Coinbase Wallet (simplest), or Rainbow (mobile). On a phone, open this page inside your wallet app's browser.
  2. Get free testnet ETH on Optimism Sepolia: Alchemy OP Sepolia faucet (free account). Paste your wallet address, claim, wait a minute.
  3. Go to /mint, connect your wallet (it will switch you to the right network automatically), pick your tier, confirm. One transaction: donation + membership.
  4. You are Member #X. Your number is permanent and public on the member directory.

2 · Join the community

  1. Join the FREEDAM Discord. New arrivals start in #welcome.
  2. Get verified. DM a mod your wallet address (or your explorer link from the mint page). We check it on-chain and assign your Member or Leader role — that unlocks the rest of the server.
  3. Introduce yourself in #general: what brought you here, what you want to build.
  4. Read the rules. Non-partisan at the protocol level. One person, one membership. Running a bot or agent? Disclose it — see the Code of Conduct.

Follow @FREEDAMDAO for weekly build logs.

3 · Contribute

FREEDAM is open source (MIT). Code, design, writing, organizing — all welcome.

  • GitHub issues — look for good first issue
  • Contributing guide — fork, branch, PR
  • Constitution · Whitepaper
  • Questions: Discord #general for quick ones, GitHub issues for technical ones

Governance

FREEDAM votes on Snapshot (off-chain, gasless — you just sign a message). FRDM-ID holders decide constitution amendments, strategy, and development priorities. Proposals are discussed in Discord #governance and voted in the Snapshot space (link goes live with our first proposal — watch #info).
