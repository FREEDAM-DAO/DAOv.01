# FREEDAM DAO Constitution

*Free Decentralized Autonomous Movement — "Dissent Stay Decent & Decentralize"*

**Adopted:** 2026 · **Ratified by:** FRDM-ID holder vote · **Version:** 1.0

This Constitution is the founding law of FREEDAM. Where it conflicts with the Whitepaper, this document governs. The Whitepaper remains the explanatory and strategic companion text.

---

## Article I — Purpose

FREEDAM is a non-partisan, open-source DAO that restores democratic participation through blockchain-native governance. It exists to let individuals organize, propose, vote, and fund civic causes without intermediaries.

The DAO is explicitly civic. It is not a DeFi protocol, a speculative venture, or a political party.

## Article II — Core Principles

1. **Permissionless participation.** Any individual may mint a soulbound FRDM-ID and join. No gatekeeper, no fee at launch.
2. **One person, one voice.** Governance rights are tied to identity, not capital. Soulbound design prevents the sale of influence.
3. **Anti-plutocratic voting.** Quadratic weighting limits the power of large holdings.
4. **Transparent execution.** Every governance decision and treasury movement is publicly verifiable on-chain.
5. **Non-partisan at the protocol level.** The DAO endorses no candidate, party, or campaign.
6. **Open source, forkable.** All code and documents are published under MIT. The DAO is designed to be copied.
7. **Fair launch.** No presale, no VC allocation, no founder reserve. FRDM is participation-mined only.

## Article III — Membership

- Membership is the soulbound ERC-1155 **FRDM-ID**.
- Minting is free at launch; non-transferable by design.
- Revocation requires a passed DAO vote in verified cases of bad faith.
- Abstracted-identity verification may be added in Phase 2 to resist Sybil attacks; it can not monetize or arbitrarily restrict entry for people.

## Article IV — Governance

Governance follows the lifecycle: **Draft → Review → Snapshot Vote → Execution → Accountability.**

1. **Draft.** Any FRDM-ID holder may submit a proposal with rationale, budget (if any), and success criteria.
2. **Review.** 72-hour community discussion on Discord + governance forum.
3. **Vote.** 5-day gasless Snapshot vote. Quorum: 10% of active FRDM-ID holders.
4. **Execution.** Approved proposals execute on-chain via Gnosis Safe + Tally.
5. **Accountability.** Funded proposals report outcomes on-chain within 90 days.

**Voting power** = √(FRDM held + XP earned).  
**Treasury** = Gnosis Safe multisig, 3-of-5 signers, geographically distributed and identity-verified.

## Article V — Token Architecture

| Token | Standard | Role | Transferable |
|-------|----------|------|--------------|
| FRDM-ID | ERC-1155 | Membership + proposal rights | No (soulbound) |
| FRDM | ERC-20 | Earned participation; QV weight | Yes, but never sold or pre-mined |

FRDM is earned through verified civic engagement: proposals passed (+10), votes cast (+1 each), IRL attendance (+25), verified referrals (+15). No maximum supply; issuance tied to participation and adjustable by vote.

## Article VI — Representation

Any FRDM-ID holder may nominate a Representative to advocate for the DAO externally (media, legislature, forums). Representatives are elected by DAO vote, serve fixed terms, and are accountable on-chain. A Representative may be recalled by a passed vote.

## Article VII — Non-Partisan Constraint

The DAOs purpose is NOT to:
- Endorse or oppose candidates or parties.
- Make campaign contributions.
- Coordinate with political parties as an organization.

Proposals that constitute illegal political activity are rejected and referred for legal review. Individual members remain free to act as private citizens.

## Article VIII — Legal Structure

The DAO operates without a fixed legal wrapper until the membership votes one in as a first-order governance action. Evaluating options: Wyoming DAO LLC, Open Source Collective (501(c)(6)), Delaware 501(c)(4), Unincorporated Non-Profit Association. Until then, the DAO functions as an open-source community governed by this Constitution.

## Article IX — Amendments

This Constitution is a living document. Any Article may be amended by the standard governance process (Article IV), with two additions:
- Amendment proposals require a **supermajority of 66%** of votes cast (higher than ordinary proposals).
- Ratified amendments are recorded with date and vote ID in the appendix below.

## Article X — Supersession

In any conflict between this Constitution, the Whitepaper, or community guidelines, this Constitution prevails. When legal structure achieves community support and is established it must include the FREEDAM DAO constitution.
